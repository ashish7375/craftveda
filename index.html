Frequently asked questions

Everything worth knowing before you order.

Need more help?

Message us directly. We usually reply the same day.

Message on WhatsApp
Are the artworks original?

Yes. Every artwork is handmade in our studio. We do not sell prints or reproductions.

How long does delivery take?

Once dispatched, delivery takes 8 to 10 days depending on your destination. The dispatch time is confirmed when you order, because every artwork is handmade.

Can I preview a piece in my room?

Yes. Send a photo of your wall on WhatsApp and we will share a mockup showing the artwork in your space.

How does custom art work?

Share your size, colours and idea. You receive a design idea and price, and work begins after you approve and pay an advance.

How do I pay?

Payment is arranged through UPI or a secure payment link, shared on WhatsApp once your order is confirmed.

What if my artwork arrives damaged?

Send photos and an unboxing video within 24 hours of delivery, and we will repair it and send it again.
